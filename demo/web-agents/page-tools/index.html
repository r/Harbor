Chapter One

The ferry left at six, as it always did, carrying the same handful of commuters across the grey water.

Mara counted the gulls on the railing and lost track at eleven, which she took as a sign of something.

By the time they reached the far side, the fog had lifted enough to show the lighthouse, white and patient.

At night the harbor turned into a field of small lights, each one a boat that had chosen to stay.

She walked home along the seawall, thinking about the letter she still had not opened.
